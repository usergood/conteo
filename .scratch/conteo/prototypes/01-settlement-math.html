Settlement math checker

At month close you type the exact MXN that hit your CitiBancoMex account. The app already knows the month's USD paid and your settings (bank %, fixed fee, tax %). Is the derived bank rate correct — for a single transfer, several transfers, and the "one transfer vs per-project transfers" choice at close? Drive it with your real bank statement numbers and see.

App derives the bank's effective rate: rate = (typed MXN + transfers × fixed fee) ÷ (USD paid × (1 − bank %))
